## Why

The Growth tab (change `growth-tracking`) lists each measurement with its own percentile
bars, which answers "where was she on that day" one row at a time. A parent following
growth wants the picture a clinic chart gives: the child's measurements drawn as a line
across the WHO percentile curves, so a steady track along one percentile and a drift
across several are visible at a glance. The `growth-tracking` proposal deferred exactly
this as its first non-goal ("Charts — a line chart against the WHO curves").

**Scope: [Stretch]. Post-MVP follow-on to `growth-tracking` (Day 11 in the project's day
numbering); apply only after `growth-tracking` is committed, since it builds on its
routes, types, and tab.** No new model call, no new dependency: the curves are table
arithmetic from the committed WHO LMS parameters and the charts are inline SVG.

## What Changes

- **WHO percentile reference curves per indicator** — a new owner-scoped read,
  `GET /api/children/{id}/growth/curves`, returns, for the child's sex, the 10th through
  90th percentile curves at every tenth percentile for each of the four indicators:
  weight-for-age, length/height-for-age, and BMI-for-age over months 0–60, and
  weight-for-length/height over the centimetre range of the table the child's current age
  uses. Every value comes from the same LMS parameters and arithmetic `assess_growth`
  reads; nothing is generated by the model (architecture principle; hard rule 5 provenance
  carried on the response).
- **Exact age for plotting** — each growth measurement in the list gains
  `age_months_exact`, the age on the measurement day in months to two decimals, so
  points within the same completed month spread along the x-axis. The assessment itself
  still uses completed months (hard rule 1 unchanged: age is months).
- **Four line charts on the Growth tab** — one per indicator, above the list, each drawing
  the nine percentile curves (thin, muted; the 50th thicker and darker), the child's
  measurements as a line with markers, direct labels on the 10th, 50th, and 90th curves,
  recessive axes and grid, and a hover tooltip on each marker naming the date, the value,
  and the percentile. Age charts span 0–24 months for a child under 24 months and 0–60
  otherwise; the weight-for-length/height chart spans the table's range. Measurements
  without a length/height appear only on the weight-for-age chart. Charts show once at
  least one measurement exists; the list stays as the table view.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

_None._

### Modified Capabilities

- `growth-tracking`: a new requirement — percentile reference curves for the child's sex
  are available per indicator, computed by arithmetic from the WHO standards, and each
  measurement carries its exact age in months for plotting. (Additive; the capability is
  introduced by the unarchived `growth-tracking` change.)
- `chat-ui`: a new requirement — the Growth tab plots each indicator as a line chart of
  the child's measurements over the WHO percentile curves, with the 50th percentile
  emphasised. (Additive to the Growth-tab requirement `growth-tracking` adds.)

## Impact

- **Backend**: `backend/nutrition/growth.py` gains the inverse-LMS value-at-percentile and
  a cached `reference_curves(sex, use_height_table)` builder; `backend/schemas/tools.py`
  gains the curve models; `backend/schemas/child.py` adds `age_months_exact` to the
  measurement response; `backend/api/children.py` adds one `GET` route behind
  `OwnedChild`; `tests/test_app.py` route surface, `tests/test_nutrition_growth.py`,
  `tests/test_api_children.py` extended.
- **Frontend**: new `components/side-panel/growth-charts.tsx` (inline SVG, no library),
  `hooks/use-growth-log.ts` gains `useGrowthCurves`, `growth-tab.tsx` renders the charts,
  `api-types.ts` and `copy.ts` (+ snapshot) extended; `use-child-context.ts` and the
  sign-out/401 paths drop the `["growth-curves"]` cache with the other account-scoped
  caches.
- **API contract**: additive only — one new route and one new response field.
- **Data / security**: no schema change; the new read is owner-scoped (hard rule 9);
  nothing new reaches the language model.
- **Dependencies**: none.

## Non-goals

- **Charting library** — four small SVG charts do not justify one; if the charts grow
  (zoom, brushing, print export) revisit. [Stretch]
- **WHO's ±2 SD / ±3 SD z-score curves** — the parent asked for percentiles; the z-score
  lines are a different convention and would double the curve count. [Stretch]
- **Plotting a child across the 24-month table change** — the weight-for-length/height
  chart uses one table (length under 24 months, height from 24), chosen by the child's
  current age; older points measured against the other table are still plotted by their
  centimetres, and the chart says which table it shows.
- **Gestational-age correction, target percentiles, velocity** — unchanged from the
  growth-assessment non-goals.
- **Table view as a separate toggle** — the existing newest-first list under the charts is
  the accessible table view.

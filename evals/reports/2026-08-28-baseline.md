## Notes (author, at baseline)

**Headline.** On this test set the two strategies are indistinguishable on retrieval and
routing (every deterministic check ties except one routing flake, below) and within noise on
generation: `advanced` is marginally more faithful (+0.014, 5 wins / 4 losses) and marginally
lower on context precision and recall (−0.028 / −0.021), while costing ~1.3 s more median
latency (4.0 s vs 2.7 s) for the extra translation call. Nothing here argues for changing
the default; it does argue that the corpus is small and well-labelled enough that a single
query already finds the right document — which is what 100% `retrieval_hit` on both sides
says. A Day 7 decision on the default strategy should weigh latency against the faithfulness
edge, not retrieval.

**Failed checks, explained.** None is a test-set error; all are findings about the system.

- `rag-vegan-toddler` (both strategies) — the answer lists protein, iron, zinc, calcium and
  vitamin D but omits **vitamin B12**, which the source document
  (`aap-vegetarian-and-vegan-diets`) calls out first and most strongly ("all vegan children
  need a B12 supplement"). Retrieval found the document; generation dropped its lead point.
  Worth a look at the answer prompt's handling of enumerations — after the eval, since prompts
  are frozen for this baseline.
- `tool-meal-nutrition` (both strategies) — asked for the nutrition of "150 ml of
  iron-fortified infant formula plus 30 g of iron-fortified infant cereal", the router calls
  `lookup_food` twice with free-text names, both fail (the table is keyed by ids like
  `infant_formula_iron_fortified`), and `calculate_meal_nutrition` is never chosen. The tool
  description or the router prompt should steer "what's in X plus Y" to the meal-nutrition
  tool and give the model a way to resolve names to ids (the `/api/foods` listing exists but
  the router does not see it).
- `tool-food-lookup` (`advanced` only; passed on `basic` and in both earlier deterministic
  passes) — "What nutrients are in a serving of avocado from your food table?" was routed to
  the knowledge base and hit the no-answer message. A routing flake of the kind the Day 5
  notes record; the rate over four runs is 1/8.

**Method caveats.** One judge pass with the application's own chat model
(`openai/gpt-4o-mini`) — a self-preference bias the header records; set `EVAL_JUDGE_MODEL`
to a stronger model to re-run. 28 judged cases per strategy; ties within ±0.05 are noise. The
`hybrid` category's low faithfulness (~0.56) is largely an artefact of scoring a meal plan's
tool-generated numbers against retrieved guideline passages that do not contain them.
